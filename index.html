Encore is software for buying tickets on official platforms. It joins the ticket sale at the scheduled time, takes a place in the online queue under your own account and adds matching tickets to the cart. The purchase and payment are completed by you.

  1. Apply. Answer four short questions and verify your Discord account.
  2. Review. Our team reviews the application, usually within 24 hours.
  3. Pay. Once approved, you get a link to the dashboard by Discord DM and pay there in USDT or USDC. An invoice is issued for every payment.
  4. Start. Your license key is in the dashboard, ready to use.
